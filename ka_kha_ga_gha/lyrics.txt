Core Direction

Genre: Nepali preschool alphabet chant pop with xylophone and ukulele
Tempo: 116 BPM
Taal: 4/4 straight bouncy groove, claps and light madal
Length: 3:00
Key: D Major
Lead voice: bright confident girl (about 8 years old) leads; small children's choir repeats
Lesson: the first ten consonants क ख ग घ ङ च छ ज झ ञ with one object word each
Mood: letter chant → objects pop in one by one → humming ङ game → slow whisper recap → loud proud finale

THE NEW WAY: The two-line letter chant stays; the children's answer grows: अक्षर साथी! → क ख ग! → च छ ज! → सबै अक्षर! Every verse line is one letter + one object the child can point to.

How it must sound:
- Xylophone plays the letter tune first;
- ukulele and claps carry the verses, madal only in choruses;
- a tiny bell on every new letter;
- the bridge is a slow whisper recap over soft piano, then one bar of silence;
- the final chorus is loud and proud with the full choir.
