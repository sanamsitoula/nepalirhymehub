🔢 एक दुई तीन, गन्ती गन — EK DUI TEEN | NEPALI COUNTING SONG 1 TO 10
Nepali Rhyme Hub (नेपाली राइम हब) | नेपाली बालगीत | original song, lyrics and melody (2026-11-07)

Core Direction

Genre: Nepali counting game song with bansuri and marimba
Tempo: 102 BPM
Taal: 4/4 gentle bounce with damphu and soft madal
Length: 3:00
Key: G Major
Lead voice: duet: warm young man and a little girl; children's choir counts along
Lesson: numbers एक to दस with Nepali counters (एउटा, दुईटा … दसवटा) and Nepal's nature
Mood: counting chant → sun, danphe, goats → rhododendrons, butterflies, fish → stars, frogs, birds, fingers → count backwards → proud ten

THE NEW WAY: The two-line count stays; the children's answer grows: गन्ती गरौँ! → फेरि गनौँ! → तारा गनौँ! → सबै गनौँ! Each verse adds three new numbers with something from Nepal to count.

How it must sound:
- Marimba plays the counting tune and a soft tick on every number;
- bansuri answers each call;
- Verse 1 is marimba and bansuri (no madal);
- the bridge counts backwards over bansuri alone, then one bar of silence;
- the final chorus is full damphu, madal, marimba and choir.

🎼 FULL LYRICS

[Intro]

एक, दुई, तीन!

[Chorus 1]
एक दुई तीन, चार पाँच छ!

दस! दस!

[Verse 1]

एक, दुई, तीन!

[Verse 2]

[Instrumental]

[Verse 3]
