# Nepali Rhyme Hub — Modern Urban Universe Character Bible (v1, 2026)
## "हाम्रो समूह" (Hamro Squad) — Contemporary Kids of Nepal

---

## 1. Vision & Creative Philosophy

### The Strategic Shift: From Antique Villages to Contemporary Vibrant Nepal
Traditional Nepali children's channels (*Nani & Babu*, *AnimNepal*, *Puku Luku*) have historically restricted their visual universe to 1980s rural mud huts and pastoral village archetypes. While rural heritage is treasured, **over 60% of Nepali children today (in Kathmandu, Pokhara, Lalitpur, Butwal, Biratnagar, Chitwan, Dharan, and the global Nepali diaspora in the US, UK, Australia, and Gulf) live in modern homes, attend tech-enabled schools, ride electric buses, play in city parks, skateboard, and express themselves through art, science, and music.**

**"हाम्रो समूह" (Hamro Squad)** bridges contemporary urban life with authentic cultural pride:
- **Modern Everywhere:** Sleek sneakers, bright hoodies, denim jackets, skateboards, tablets, cameras, telescopes, clean modern classrooms, solar-powered parks, and electric buses.
- **Rooted in Heritage:** Subtle, elegant traditional motifs woven naturally into modern streetwear (Dhaka pattern trims, Mithila pocket embroidery, Ashtamangala lucky knot stickers, Tamang/Sherpa woven beanies, Newari brass accents).
- **Multi-Cultural & Multi-Religious Harmony:** Children representing Nepal's geographical zones (Himalaya, Hills, Terai) and diverse communities (Newar, Tamang/Sherpa, Maithili/Madhesi, Tharu, Rai/Limbu, Muslim) exploring, learning, and celebrating together as best friends.

## 2. Character Profiles: The "Hamro Squad"

### 1. Aarav (आरभ) — Kathmandu Valley (Urban Tech & Music Explorer)
- **Age:** 7 years old
- **City / Region:** Kathmandu (Baneshwor / Durbar Marg)
- **Cultural Heritage:** Urban Newar / Brahmin contemporary family
- **Visual Appearance:**
  - **Hair & Face:** Messy, stylish modern dark brown hair with a bright, curious smile and expressive hazel eyes.
  - **Outfit:** Vibrant mustard/electric yellow hoodie with subtle red-and-black Dhaka trim on the hood strings; dark navy denim joggers; glowing white-and-teal LED high-top sneakers.
  - **Signature Accessories:** Lightweight wireless cyan headphones resting around his neck; mini digital beatpad or smart watch on his wrist.
- **Personality & Role:**
  - Curious, tech-savvy, energetic, rhythm enthusiast.
  - He loves beatboxing, programming small robot toys, and mixing traditional Madal beats with electronic keyboard melodies.
- **Catchphrase:** *"वाह! यो त कति गज्जब छ!" (Wow! This is amazing!)*

- **Age:** 8 years old
- **City / Region:** Lalitpur (Patan Durbar / Jhamsikhel art district)
- **Cultural Heritage:** Newari artisan family
- **Visual Appearance:**
  - **Hair & Face:** Glossy dark hair tied in a high, bouncy ponytail with a crimson silk scrunchie; bright, observant almond-shaped eyes; rosy cheeks.
  - **Outfit:** Cropped light-wash denim jacket with delicate red lotus flower embroidery on the back; layered over a pastel coral-pink modern cotton kurti-dress; clean white sneakers with multi-colored pastel laces.

  - Creative, perceptive, thoughtful, deeply artistic.
  - She notices architectural beauty everywhere—from ancient Patan pagoda roofs and carved wood struts to modern city murals and eco-friendly architecture.
- **Catchphrase:** *"रङ्ग भरौँ, नयाँ संसार बनाऔँ!" (Let's paint colors and create a new world!)*

## 3. Visual Styling & CGI Rendering Rules

| Element | Specification | What to Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| **Animation Style** | 3D Stylized CGI (Pixar / Disney / Illumination quality), warm organic lighting, soft bevels, expressive faces. | Stiff 2D flash rigs, eerie photorealistic uncanny valley, or low-poly untextured models. |
| **Color Palette** | Night Blue (`#14234B`), Marigold (`#F7A21B`), Diyo Gold (`#FFD166`), Sindoor Crimson (`#C8102E`), Teal (`#17C3B2`), Emerald (`#2A9D8F`). | Dull muddy greys, desaturated tones, fluorescent neon clashes. |
| **Lighting** | Golden hour sunlight, clean morning blue sky, vibrant festival diyo warmth, subtle modern LED city glows. | Dark gloomy shadows, overcast horror tones, harsh flat lighting. |
| **Cityscapes** | Clean, wide paved pedestrian plazas, electric buses, modern street lamps, rooftop gardens, historic pagoda temples nestled organically beside clean modern glass cafes. | Grimy traffic jams, smoggy pollution, decaying infrastructure, dangerous open wiring. |
| **Cultural Fusion** | Everyday modern wear accented with authentic cultural patterns (Dhaka, Mithila, Buddhist flags, Tharu geometry). | Caricaturish heavy historical costumes for everyday play, mismatched ceremonial gear. |
