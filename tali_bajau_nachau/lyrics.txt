👏 ताली ताली ताली बजाऊ — TALI BAJAU NACHAU | NEPALI ACTION DANCE SONG
Nepali Rhyme Hub (नेपाली राइम हब) | नेपाली बालगीत | original song, lyrics and melody (2026-11-07)

Core Direction

Genre: Nepali Jhyaure 6/8 action dance song with madal and sarangi
Tempo: 124 BPM
Taal: 6/8 Jhyaure on madal with damphu accents
Length: 3:00
Key: A Major
Lead voice: cheerful young man, playful and energetic; children's choir shouts the body-part answers
Lesson: body parts (हात, खुट्टा, काँध, टाउको, आँखा) and movement; make room for friends while dancing
Mood: clap call → hands → feet → head and shoulders → slow breathing → FREEZE game in the silent bar → biggest dance

THE NEW WAY: The call "ताली ताली ताली बजाऊ, उफ्री उफ्री नाच!" stays; the children's answer names the next body part: ताली! → खुट्टा! → टाउको! → सबै सँगै! The bridge's bar of silence is a freeze game: children stop still, then the final chorus explodes.

How it must sound:
- Three hand claps and a madal roll open the song;
- sarangi plays the dance tune and answers every call;
- Verse 1 is claps and sarangi only (no madal);
- the bridge is soft sarangi and breath, "रोकिऊ!", then one bar of silence (the freeze);
- the final chorus is full madal, damphu, claps and the whole choir.
