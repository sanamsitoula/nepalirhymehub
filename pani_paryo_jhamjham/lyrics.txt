Nepali Rhyme Hub (नेपाली राइम हब) | नेपाली बालगीत | original song, lyrics and melody (2026-11-07)

Core Direction

Genre: Nepali hill-folk rain-play nursery pop
Tempo: 108 BPM
Taal: 4/4 bouncy Keherwa on madal, rain-shaker and claps
Length: 3:00
Key: G Major
Lead voice: warm young woman (kindergarten teacher), smiling; children's choir answers
Lesson: rain words (बादल, थोपा, खोल्सा, इन्द्रेणी), play happily and change into dry clothes after
Mood: clouds over the hills → first drop → paper boats in puddles → friendly cloud drum → sun and rainbow

THE NEW WAY: The call "झम झम पानी, छम छम नानी!" stays; the children's answer grows each chorus: झम झम! → छप छप! → गड गड! → इन्द्रेणी आयो! Each answer is a sound children can copy with their hands.

How it must sound:
- Rain-shaker and soft glockenspiel drops open the song (टप टप);
- nylon guitar and a light bansuri phrase answer every call;
- madal enters on Chorus 1, rests in Verse 1;
- the bridge is bansuri and glockenspiel only, then one bar of silence;
- the final chorus bursts with madal, claps, glockenspiel and the whole choir.

[Verse 1]
