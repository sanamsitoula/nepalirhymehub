Core Direction

Genre: Nepali Deusi call-and-response march with madal, jhyamta and a boys' chorus
Tempo: 118 BPM
Taal: 4/4 marching Deusi groove on madal with jhyamta cymbals
Length: 3:00
Key: D Major
Lead voice: a confident, cheerful 8-9 year old Nepali boy (a real child voice); boys' chorus shouts देउसी रे
Lesson: Govardhan Puja night: Krishna lifting Govardhan, worship of oxen, blessings for every home, and giving the Deusi money to the village school
Mood: jhyamta clash → Govardhan story → blessings at a home → the community gift → quiet blessing, silence → biggest village march

THE NEW WAY: The call "भन मेरा भाइ हो, देउसी रे!" stays; the fourth line grows: देउसी रे! → आशीष दिऔँ! → बाँडेर खाऔँ! → शुभ तिहार! Hindu details checked (Tihar 2083 = Nov 7-11, 2026): Kag Tihar (crows fed rice), Kukur Tihar (dogs get a garland, tika and food), Gai Tihar + Laxmi Puja on Kartik Aunsi (cow worshipped, house plastered with cow dung, red footprints of Laxmi from the door to the puja room, diyo lamps; girls sing Bhailo), Govardhan Puja / Goru Tihar (Krishna lifted Govardhan to shelter Gokul; oxen worshipped; boys sing Deusi), Bhai Tika. Vegetarian only: sel roti, fruit, rice, nuts. Traditional folk elements used: the response देउसी रे, the call भन मेरा भाइ हो and the idea that Deusi singers are sent by King Bali (बलि राजा). Every line is newly written.

How it must sound:
- Jhyamta clash and madal in the first second;
- every verse line ends with the children's shout देउसी रे;
- Verse 1 and 3 drop the madal; 
- the bridge is bansuri and harmonium only, then one bar of silence and a madal pickup;
- the final chorus is the whole village marching.
