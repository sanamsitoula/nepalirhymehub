Core Direction

Genre: Stomping Nepali Deusi knock-and-shout march with madal, jhyamta and a boys' chorus
Tempo: 118 BPM
Taal: 4/4 marching Deusi groove on madal with jhyamta cymbals
Length: 3:00
Key: D Major
Lead voice: a confident, cheerful 8-9 year old Nepali boy (a real child voice); boys' chorus shouts देउसी रे
Lesson: Govardhan Puja night: Krishna lifting Govardhan, worship of oxen, blessings for every home, and giving the Deusi money to the village school
Mood: door-knock hook → Krishna holds up Govardhan in the Gokul rain → blessings sent by King Bali → the gift for the village school → three soft knocks, silence → the biggest village march

THE NEW WAY: NEW HOOK (v2): a percussive knock chant "ढक ढक ढक, ढोका खोल, देउसी आयो!" — three knock sounds on the jhyamta, answered by the boys "देउसी रे! देउसी रे!". It sounds nothing like the Bhailo sparkle chant. The fourth line grows each chorus: हो! हो! हो! → आशीष ल्यायो! → खुसी ल्यायो! → तिहार ल्यायो! Hindu details checked (Tihar 2083 = Nov 7-11, 2026): Kag Tihar (crows fed rice), Kukur Tihar (dogs get a garland, tika and food), Gai Tihar + Laxmi Puja on Kartik Aunsi (cow worshipped, house plastered with cow dung, red footprints of Laxmi from the door to the puja room, diyo lamps; girls sing Bhailo), Govardhan Puja / Goru Tihar (Krishna lifted Govardhan to shelter Gokul; oxen worshipped; boys sing Deusi), Bhai Tika. Vegetarian only: sel roti, fruit, rice, nuts. Traditional elements kept: the response देउसी रे and the singers sent by King Bali. Every line is new.

How it must sound:
- Jhyamta clash and madal in the first second;
- every verse line ends with the children's shout देउसी रे;
- Verse 1 and 3 drop the madal; 
- the bridge is bansuri and harmonium only, then one bar of silence and a madal pickup;
- the final chorus is the whole village marching.
