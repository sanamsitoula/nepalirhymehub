# मादल बज्यो ठमठम (Madal Bajyo Thamtham)
# Nepali Rhyme Hub — Flagship Album Lead Single

## 1. Musical Production Roadmap
- Key: D Major (bright, cheerful, singable for toddlers)
- Tempo: 115 BPM (lively 4/4 Keherwa rhythm)
- Main Instruments: Traditional Nepali Madal (distinct Daayan and Baayan acoustics), Bansuri (Nepali bamboo flute), Acoustic Guitar, Glockenspiel bells, rhythmic handclaps
- Vocal Architecture: Warm, joyful lead vocal (child educator) + enthusiastic children's choir (नानी-बाबु समूह)
- Singable Syllable Pace: 4.4 characters per second (ideal for ages 1–8)

Daayaan haanda ghanana bajyo, baayaan haanda dham!

---

## 4. English Summary & Toddler Actions
- Meaning: A joyful celebration of Nepal's iconic folk percussion instrument—the Madal. Children learn the parts of the madal (carved wood, leather skin, black tuning khari), its two contrasting tones (high pitch 'dha' and deep bass 'dham'), and how music unites friends from all cultural communities.
- Interactive Actions:
  1. Patting hands alternately on thighs to mimic playing the two sides of a madal (*दायाँ-बायाँ*).
  2. Jumping and spinning with rhythmic clapping (*छम छम*).
  3. Whispering during the bridge, then leaping up for the grand final chorus.
