## 1. Search Intent & Keyword Gap Analysis (gl=NP, YouTube Search)
- **High-Demand Target Queries**: "aama baba pyaro ghar", "nepali rhymes for children", "balgeet nepali", "nepali kids songs", "nepali nursery rhymes".
