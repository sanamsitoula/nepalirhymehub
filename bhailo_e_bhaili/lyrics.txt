Core Direction

Genre: Sparkling Nepali Bhailo folk-pop in swinging 6/8 with madal, sarangi and a girls' chorus
Tempo: 100 BPM
Taal: 6/8 swinging Nepali folk groove on madal, jhyali and claps
Length: 3:00
Key: A Major
Lead voice: a sweet, playful 7-8 year old Nepali girl (a real child voice, never an adult imitating one); girls' chorus answers
Lesson: Laxmi Puja night: Bhailo from house to house, Laxmi's footprints, diyo, marigold garlands, kindness to crows, dogs and cows, sharing
Mood: sparkle chant → Laxmi's footsteps on Aunsi night → house to house with sel roti → animals of Tihar → whisper question game, silence → the whole village sings the sparkle chant

THE NEW WAY: NEW HOOK (v2): a 2-bar sparkle chant "झिलिमिली! झिलिमिली! भैलो आयो, भैलो!" — two repeated sound words + one shout, sung by the girl in the first second, answered by the girls "भैलो! भैलो!". The fourth line grows each chorus: आउनुहोस्! → फूलै फूल! → मीठो मीठो! → भैलो! भैलो! भैलो! Hindu details checked (Tihar 2083 = Nov 7-11, 2026): Kag Tihar (crows fed rice), Kukur Tihar (dogs get a garland, tika and food), Gai Tihar + Laxmi Puja on Kartik Aunsi (cow worshipped, house plastered with cow dung, red footprints of Laxmi from the door to the puja room, diyo lamps; girls sing Bhailo), Govardhan Puja / Goru Tihar (Krishna lifted Govardhan to shelter Gokul; oxen worshipped; boys sing Deusi), Bhai Tika. Vegetarian only: sel roti, fruit, rice, nuts. Traditional line kept: हरियो गोबरले लिपेको, लक्ष्मी पूजा गरेको. Every other line is new.

How it must sound:
- No long intro: madal pattern and the girl's shout in the first 3 seconds;
- sarangi and bansuri answer every call;
- slow, soft verses and explosive choruses;
- the bridge is the girl alone with sarangi, one bar of silence, then a madal pickup;
- the final chorus is the whole village singing.
