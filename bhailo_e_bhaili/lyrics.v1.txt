Core Direction

Genre: Modern Nepali Bhailo folk with live madal, sarangi and a girls' village chorus
Tempo: 100 BPM
Taal: 6/8 swinging Nepali folk groove on madal, jhyali and claps
Length: 3:00
Key: A Major
Lead voice: a sweet, playful 7-8 year old Nepali girl (a real child voice, never an adult imitating one); girls' chorus answers
Lesson: Laxmi Puja night: Bhailo from house to house, Laxmi's footprints, diyo, marigold garlands, kindness to crows, dogs and cows, sharing
Mood: madal burst → evening Laxmi Puja → house to house with blessings → animals of Tihar → one diyo each, silence → whole village dance

THE NEW WAY: The call "ए भैली! ए भैली!" stays; the children's answer grows: है! है! भैली! → दियो बल्यो! → सेलरोटी! → शुभ तिहार! Hindu details checked (Tihar 2083 = Nov 7-11, 2026): Kag Tihar (crows fed rice), Kukur Tihar (dogs get a garland, tika and food), Gai Tihar + Laxmi Puja on Kartik Aunsi (cow worshipped, house plastered with cow dung, red footprints of Laxmi from the door to the puja room, diyo lamps; girls sing Bhailo), Govardhan Puja / Goru Tihar (Krishna lifted Govardhan to shelter Gokul; oxen worshipped; boys sing Deusi), Bhai Tika. Vegetarian only: sel roti, fruit, rice, nuts. The line हरियो गोबरले लिपेको, लक्ष्मी पूजा गरेको is the traditional Bhailo opening phrase (folk, public domain); every other line is new.

How it must sound:
- No long intro: madal pattern and the girl's shout in the first 3 seconds;
- sarangi and bansuri answer every call;
- slow, soft verses and explosive choruses;
- the bridge is the girl alone with sarangi, one bar of silence, then a madal pickup;
- the final chorus is the whole village singing.
