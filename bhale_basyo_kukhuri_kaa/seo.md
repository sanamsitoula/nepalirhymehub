## 1. Search Intent & Keyword Gap Analysis (gl=NP, YouTube Search)
- **High-Demand Target Queries**: "bhale basyo kukhuri kaa", "nepali rhymes for children", "balgeet nepali", "nepali kids songs", "nepali nursery rhymes".
- **Competitor Flaw**: Competitors use flat synthesized MIDI sounds with no cultural grounding. Our live authentic Rooster Crow SFX, Upbeat Madal, Bansuri, Claps and 3D Pixar/Ghibli aesthetic fill a major content void.
- **Search Intent**: Toddlers wanting to sing along and mimic actions (Flapping wings with bent elbows, stretching tall, chanting 'Kukhuri Kaa!'), and parents looking for moral, clean Nepali language education.
