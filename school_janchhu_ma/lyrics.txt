🎒 टन टन टन, स्कुलको घण्टी — SCHOOL JANCHHU MA | NEPALI MORNING ROUTINE SONG
Nepali Rhyme Hub (नेपाली राइम हब) | नेपाली बालगीत | original song, lyrics and melody (2026-11-07)

Core Direction

Genre: Nepali village-morning walking song with steel guitar and sarangi
Tempo: 112 BPM
Taal: 4/4 easy walking groove, light madal and footsteps
Length: 3:00
Key: C Major
Lead voice: happy young boy (about 7) leads; friends' choir answers the checklist
Lesson: morning routine (उठ्ने, दाँत माझ्ने, नमस्ते), packing the bag, greeting the teacher, sharing food
Mood: school bell → wake up → pack the bag → walk with friends → learn and share → home → 'tomorrow again'

THE NEW WAY: The call "टन टन टन, स्कुलको घण्टी!" stays; the answer is a checklist that grows: तयार? हो! → झोला? छ! → पढ्न! खेल्न! → भोलि फेरि! Children answer like a real morning check.

How it must sound:
- A small brass school bell opens the song (टन टन टन);
- steel guitar strums the walking groove, sarangi answers each call;
- Verse 1 is guitar and birds only (no madal);
- the bridge is soft sarangi and evening birds, then one bar of silence;
- the final chorus is full madal, claps, bell and the whole choir.
