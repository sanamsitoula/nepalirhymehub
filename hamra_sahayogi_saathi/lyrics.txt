================================================================================
1. CORE DIRECTION
================================================================================

Song Title: हाम्रा सहयोगी साथी (Hamra Sahayogi Saathi)
English Title: Our Community Helpers & Cultural Village
Channel: Nepali Rhyme Hub (नेपाली राइम हब)
Genre: Upbeat Nepali Acoustic Cultural Folk-Pop Nursery Song
Tempo: 112 BPM (Steady, marchable, toddler-danceable)
Taal: 4/4 Bouncy Keherwa groove on Madal with folk syncopation & gentle claps
Length: 3:00
Key: D Major (Open, bright, joyful, warm)
Lead Voice: Warm, smiling young female educator (Auntie / Guruma voice) with clear Nepali enunciation
Child Co-Lead: Aarav & Aarya (curious, smiling 4-year-old child voices)
Chorus: Children's Choir ("नानी-बाबु समूह") answering in energetic call-and-response
Target Audience: Toddlers and young children (ages 1–8), preschool teachers, and global Nepali diaspora families
Instruments:
- Lead Rhythm: Acoustic Nepali Madal (मादल) with crisp thekka (धिन-ताक-धिन-ता)
- Melody & Accents: Bansuri (बाँसुरी), Tamang Damphu (डम्फू), Newari Dhime & Bhuchhya accents, Rai/Limbu Jhyamta (झ्याम्टा)
- Kids Pop Sparkle: Glockenspiel/Xylophone drops, acoustic nylon guitar, rhythmic handclaps, and warm round bass

Core Educational & Moral Values:
1. Respect for Work (श्रमको सम्मान): Honouring every profession equally — Farmer (किसान), Doctor/Nurse (स्वास्थ्यकर्मी), Teacher (शिक्षक), Builder/Artisan (डकर्मी/कालिगड), Sanitation Hero (सफाइकर्मी).
2. Cultural Diversity & Unity (एकता र सद्भाव): Celebrating Nepal's rich mosaic — Newari (Dhime & Yomari), Tamang (Damphu & Sonam Lhosar), Madhesi/Mithila (Mithila art & Chhath Puja), Rai/Limbu (Chandi Nach/Sakela & Udhauli), Tharu (Maghi & Danda Nach).
3. Social Habits: Hand-clapping, greeting with respectful Namaste (नमस्ते), teamwork, keeping surroundings clean, and saying "धन्यवाद" (Thank You).

Emotional Tone: 100% positive, warm, empowering, festive, and emotionally safe. Zero frightening or negative elements.

================================================================================

Joyful Nepali children's community helpers and cultural unity song, 112 BPM, bouncy 4/4 Keherwa groove on live acoustic Madal, D major. Bright acoustic nylon guitar rhythm, cheerful bansuri folk melodies, playful Damphu accents, sparkling glockenspiel bells, shakers, and syncopated children handclaps. A warm young female educator sings the melodic call with an affectionate smile; an enthusiastic Nepali children's choir ("नानी-बाबु समूह") shouts back joyful call-and-response answers. Sing in pure, clear, melodious NEPALI (नेपाली), with authentic Nepali pronunciation, not Hindi. Short, singable lines at 4.2 to 4.8 characters per second pace. Verse 1 is light with no madal, featuring only guitar, bansuri, and soft claps. The bridge features an atmospheric cultural showcase dropping drums for bansuri, damphu, and glockenspiel, followed by ONE FULL BAR OF SILENCE before a massive celebratory madal pickup into the grand final chorus. Pristine audiophile mix with -14 LUFS mastering headroom, warm punchy low-end, zero distortion.

  Full groove with added tambourine and lively handclaps. Children's response: "सबै हाम्रा साथी! / नमस्ते! नमस्ते!".
- [2:14 - 2:38] Bridge (इन्द्रेणी नेपाल — हाम्रा चाडपर्व र सांस्कृतिक एकता):

  At [2:36 - 2:38]: ONE FULL BAR OF DRAMATIC SILENCE (a gentle breath), then a rolling Madal pickup roll: धिन-ताक-धिन-ता!
- [2:38 - 2:52] Final Chorus (The Grand Celebration):
  The loudest, happiest, most celebratory burst (+0.5 dB lift). Every instrument active: Madal, Damphu, Dhime accents, Jhyamta, Bansuri, Glockenspiel, and the entire children's choir singing together at the top of their lungs with joyful clapping.
- [2:52 - 3:00] Outro:
  Bansuri signature flourish, fading shakers and chime bells. Children laugh and call out together: "सबै साथीलाई धेरै धेरै धन्यवाद! नमस्ते!".

8. CLASSROOM & PARENT INTERACTIVE PLAY GUIDE
================================================================================

1. Chorus Hook: Children hold hands in a circle and sway side to side ("हातेमालो गरौँ नानी"), then bow with palms pressed in Namaste and clap 3 times ("नमस्ते! नमस्ते!").
2. Verse 1 (Farmer): Children mimic digging with a hoe, wiping sweat from forehead with a smile, and holding up a basket of vegetables.
3. Verse 2 (Health & Teacher): Children pretend to hold a stethoscope to listen to heartbeat ("थुक-थुक"), then point to an imaginary blackboard writing letters in the air.
4. Verse 3 (Builder & Sanitation): Children mimic tapping with a light toy hammer, shaping clay with rolling hands, and rhythmic sweeping motions.
5. Bridge (Cultural Dance Steps):
   - Dhime & Yomari: Mimic beating a big two-sided drum with hands.
   - Damphu & Selo: Tap the palm on the side like holding a round frame drum.
   - Mithila & Chhath: Lift two cupped hands toward the rising sun in Arghya offering.
   - Sakela / Chandi Nach: Gentle forward step and arm wave mimicking birds in nature.
   - Tharu Danda Nach: Tapping two rhythm sticks together.
