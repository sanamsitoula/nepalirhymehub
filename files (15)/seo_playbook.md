## Market (verified)
- Puku Luku founders said in 2017 quality Nepali kids content was scarce; searches
  returned mostly Hindi rhymes. Gap = quality, wholesome, animated Nepali.
- Working formats: recurring animated characters (Nani & Babu), alphabet series
  (Puku Luku, Bal Rachana, Wings Music), lyrics on screen (eDewcate), folklore/culture.
- Benchmarks: Golu Molu 3.74M subs / 783 videos (Hindi); Khoka Puchki 678K / 454
  videos (Bengali). Winners rely on volume and a consistent style.
- Popular classics many channels cover: Meow Meow Biralo, Kukhuri ka basi bhat kha,
  Putali. High competition, so original songs + a mascot differentiate.

## Title formula (recommendation)
<Nepali title> | <English title> | Nepali Rhymes for Kids | नेपाली बालगीत
Keep the main keyword in the first ~60 characters. Always bilingual.

## Description template
1. First 2 lines: what the song is + keywords
2. Lyrics in Devanagari + Roman transliteration
3. English meaning
4. Credits
5. Playlist/subscribe links
6. Hashtags: #NepaliRhymes #नेपालीबालगीत #NepaliRhymeHub

## Starter tags
nepali rhymes, nepali bal geet, nepali nursery rhymes, nepali kids songs,
नेपाली बालगीत, बालगीत, learn nepali for kids, nepali varnamala, nepali swarmala,
nepali rhymes for toddlers, nepali baby songs, <song name>, NepaliRhymeHub

## Thumbnails
Bright high-contrast, 1-2 expressive characters, max 1-3 big Devanagari words,
same layout every video, 1280x720, under 2MB, readable on a phone.

## Content pillars
Classic rhymes | Learning (Swarmala, Varnamala, numbers, colours) |
Festival songs (Dashain, Tihar, Holi, Teej) | Original songs with mascot |
Long compilations (30-60 min)

## Upload plan
Launch with 8-10 videos, then 2-3/week at a fixed time, a Short for each chorus,
playlists per pillar, end screens, a lyrics-on-screen version.

## Compliance
Mark videos "Made for kids". Use original or licensed audio/animation; copy a
style, never another channel's assets.
