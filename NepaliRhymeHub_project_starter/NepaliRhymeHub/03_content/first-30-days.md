# First 30-Day Publishing Plan

A realistic starter cadence: one main video per week and two Shorts cut from original footage/audio. Adjust after the first month using retention and parent feedback.

| Week | Main upload | Shorts |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | म्याउँ म्याउँ बिरालो — original animal song | Cat sound chorus; guess the animal |
| 2 | एक दुई तीन चार — counting song | Count 1–5; count with claps |
| 3 | रंगीचंगी इन्द्रेणी — colors song | Name three colors; rainbow chorus |
| 4 | पानी पर्यो झमझम — rain song | Rain sound rhythm; clap-along hook |

## Production order
1. Finalize original lyrics and melody.
2. Check pronunciation and singability with a Nepali speaker.
3. Produce vocal and instrumental versions.
4. Create storyboard and original character assets.
5. Edit, mix and check captions/lyrics.
6. Prepare title, thumbnail, description, tags and playlist.
7. Upload, verify made-for-kids audience setting, credits and rights.
8. Review analytics after 7 and 28 days.
