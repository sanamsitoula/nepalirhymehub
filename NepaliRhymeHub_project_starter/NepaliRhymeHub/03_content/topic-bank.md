## Originality and child-safety checks
- Write new lyrics and melody; do not imitate a specific existing recording.
- Check the public-domain/traditional status and rights of any source material and arrangement.
- Avoid unsafe imitation, frightening imagery, stereotypes, body-shaming or commercial pressure aimed at children.
- Use age-appropriate language and a clear positive takeaway.
