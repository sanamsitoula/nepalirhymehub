# Competitor and Reference Research

Research snapshot: 7 November 2026. Public third-party analytics are estimates and can change; verify live channel figures in YouTube before using them in presentations.

## Channels to study
1. Nani & Babu — https://www.youtube.com/@naniandbabu_rhymes  
   Benchmark for recognizable characters, animated Nepali rhymes, song collections, evergreen catalogue and festival content. Third-party vidIQ snapshot dated 6 Nov 2026 reported about 2.49M subscribers and 1.06B views. These are third-party figures, not official YouTube Studio data.
2. AnimNepal Kids — https://www.youtube.com/@animnepalkids  
   Benchmark for classic rhyme collections, Nepali/English keyword variants and compilations. A surfaced collection included Intu Mintu London Ma, Kukhuri Kaa and Myau Myau Biralo.
3. Ribu Rhymes — https://www.youtube.com/@RibuRhymes  
   Benchmark for early-learning themes, alphabet, counting, animal songs and 2D/3D animation.
4. Rambabu Subedi — https://www.youtube.com/@rambabusubediofficial  
   Reference for Nepali children's poetry, vocabulary, cultural subjects and author-led children's songs.
5. Bunu Manu — https://www.youtube.com/@bunumanu  
   Reference for contemporary packaging of Nepali culture and traditional rhyme themes.
6. PANI MUNI MACHHA — https://www.youtube.com/@panimunimachha  
   Reference for Nepali children's songwriting and memorable local song concepts.

## Patterns worth testing
- Put the primary Nepali song name first in the title.
- Add a clear audience descriptor such as “नेपाली बालगीत” or “Nepali Rhymes for Kids”.
- Use one recognizable subject per thumbnail: one main character, one action, uncluttered background.
- Make compilations from a growing catalogue; include chapters/timestamps.
- Build playlists by learning goal and theme, not only upload date.
- Use seasonal songs ahead of the relevant festival, while maintaining evergreen uploads.

## Opportunity for NepaliRhymeHub
A strong gap to pursue is original, high-quality Nepali-first songs with clean diction, educational purpose, a consistent visual identity and clear parent-facing information.
