# Brand Guidelines

## Brand promise
A joyful home for original Nepali songs that children can sing and parents can trust.

## Voice
- Warm, playful, clear and respectful
- Simple Nepali vocabulary and natural pronunciation
- Gentle rather than frantic
- Educational without sounding like a lecture
- Devotional content should be respectful and age-appropriate

## Visual direction
- Bright, friendly, soft-edged 2D/3D illustration
- Nepali cultural details used thoughtfully: home, hills, village, festivals, instruments, clothing and food
- A small cast of recurring original characters
- Clear facial expressions and uncluttered scenes
- Keep character proportions, colors and clothing consistent between videos

## Thumbnail system
- One focal character
- One action or emotion
- Short Devanagari title
- Consistent logo position
- Distinct palette per content pillar, while retaining shared brand identity

## Avoid
- Copying Nani & Babu, AnimNepal Kids or other channel characters/art
- Flashing, excessive cuts, loud jump scares or overstimulating effects
- Misleading educational claims
- Stereotypes or unsafe behavior presented as fun
