## Hashtags
Use 2–3 highly relevant hashtags, e.g. #NepaliRhymes #NepaliBalgeet #NepaliKidsSongs. Do not paste a huge unrelated hashtag list.

## Thumbnail brief
- 16:9, high contrast, readable on a phone.
- One main character/subject and one clear action.
- 2–4 large Nepali words at most; avoid tiny text.
- Express the song's central idea instantly.
- Keep recurring character design and brand placement consistent.
- Do not use misleading characters, logos or copied competitor artwork.

Use actual timestamps from the final export.

## Upload checklist
- Correct audience designation (children's content must be accurately set as made for kids where applicable).
- Accurate title and description; no misleading metadata.
- Original/licensed music, vocals, animation, fonts and footage.
- Captions/lyrics proofread in Devanagari.
- End screens/cards/comments/features configured according to YouTube's made-for-kids restrictions.
- Playlist assigned; thumbnail checked at mobile size.
