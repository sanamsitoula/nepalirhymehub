# Song-to-YouTube Production Checklist

## A. Song development
- [ ] Define one age-appropriate learning/emotional objective
- [ ] Write original lyrics with a strong repeatable chorus
- [ ] Keep lines short, pronounceable and easy to remember
- [ ] Compose an original melody and arrangement
- [ ] Check Nepali grammar, spelling and diction with a fluent reviewer
- [ ] Record rights/credits for lyrics, composition, performers and samples

## B. Audio
- [ ] Clear lead vocal above accompaniment
- [ ] No clipping or harsh high frequencies
- [ ] Balanced playback on phone speakers and headphones
- [ ] Instrumental and vocal stems archived
- [ ] Final master and project files backed up

## C. Visuals
- [ ] Storyboard follows the lyric meaning
- [ ] Original characters and assets
- [ ] Consistent character model and scene continuity
- [ ] No unsafe imitation or frightening content
- [ ] Thumbnail exported and checked at small size

## D. YouTube publishing
- [ ] Accurate title, description, credits and relevant tags
- [ ] Correct made-for-kids audience setting
- [ ] Playlist and chapters where applicable
- [ ] Captions/lyrics proofread
- [ ] Rights and permissions checked
- [ ] Final video reviewed on mobile

## E. Post-publish review
- [ ] 24-hour impressions and CTR
- [ ] 7-day retention and average view duration
- [ ] 28-day returning viewers and subscriber gain
- [ ] Parent/teacher feedback and search terms
- [ ] Record one improvement for the next upload
