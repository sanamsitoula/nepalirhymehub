# Channel Strategy

## Positioning
NepaliRhymeHub is a Nepali children's music channel for playful learning, cultural connection and family sing-along time.

## Audience
- Primary: preschool and early-primary children
- Secondary: parents, caregivers, teachers and Nepali-speaking families abroad
- Language: Nepali first; add Romanized Nepali and English translations only when useful for discoverability/accessibility

## Content pillars (starting mix)
1. Traditional-inspired and original Nepali balgeet — 40%
2. Early learning: alphabet, numbers, colors, shapes — 25%
3. Animals, nature and daily life — 15%
4. Festivals and Nepali culture — 10%
5. Devotional children's songs — 10%

## Format mix
- Main song videos: 2–4 minutes initially
- Song compilations: 15–30 minutes once enough original catalogue exists
- Shorts: 15–35 seconds, one chorus, action, word or learning moment
- Lyric videos: useful for parents and teachers; keep Devanagari large and synchronized

## Differentiation
- Natural, clear Nepali pronunciation
- Original songs rooted in familiar Nepali settings and culture
- Memorable choruses children can repeat
- Consistent recurring characters and visual language
- Parent-friendly descriptions, credits and rights transparency

## Success measures
Track per upload: impressions, click-through rate, average view duration, audience retention at 0:30, returning viewers, subscribers gained, playlist starts, and comments from parents. Compare videos within the same format and topic family; do not judge Shorts and long-form by identical benchmarks.
