# NepaliRhymeHub

**Contact:** info.bhrikuty@gmail.com  
**Workspace target:** `claude_projects/NepaliRhymeHub`  
**Primary platform:** YouTube  
**Audience:** Nepali-speaking children, parents and families

## Mission
Create original, memorable Nepali children's rhymes and songs that entertain, support early learning, celebrate Nepali culture and are reassuring for parents.

## Creative reference
Use Gokul Kirtan as a reference for devotional warmth, melodic repetition and participatory singing. Use children's rhyme channels as references for clear hooks, simple language, recurring characters, playlists and family-friendly presentation.

**Originality rule:** Do not copy lyrics, melodies, arrangements, recordings, characters, artwork, thumbnails or channel branding. Create new compositions and visuals. Traditional/public-domain material still needs provenance and rights review for the specific arrangement/recording.

## Folder guide
- `01_strategy/` channel positioning and audience
- `02_research/` competitor and reference notes
- `03_content/` topic bank, calendar and song briefs
- `04_youtube_seo/` metadata templates and keyword bank
- `05_brand/` visual and voice guidelines
- `06_production/` production and publishing checklist

## First operating principle
Make every video useful without relying on excessive stimulation: clear vocals, readable Nepali, gentle pacing, safe visuals, no misleading claims, and a repeatable learning or cultural takeaway.
