## 1. Search Intent & Keyword Gap Analysis (gl=NP, YouTube Search)
- **High-Demand Target Queries**: "saat rang ko indreni", "nepali rhymes for children", "balgeet nepali", "nepali kids songs", "nepali nursery rhymes".
- **Competitor Flaw**: Competitors use flat synthesized MIDI sounds with no cultural grounding. Our live authentic Xylophone, Ukulele, Bansuri, Children's Chorus and 3D Pixar/Ghibli aesthetic fill a major content void.
- **Search Intent**: Toddlers wanting to sing along and mimic actions (Drawing a giant arch in the air with both hands, naming colors with fingers), and parents looking for moral, clean Nepali language education.
