## 1. Search Intent & Keyword Gap Analysis (gl=NP, YouTube Search)
- **High-Demand Target Queries**: "gai ko bachho", "nepali rhymes for children", "balgeet nepali", "nepali kids songs", "nepali nursery rhymes".
- **Competitor Flaw**: Competitors use flat synthesized MIDI sounds with no cultural grounding. Our live authentic Flute, Ghungroo, Soft Madal, Acoustic Guitar and 3D Pixar/Ghibli aesthetic fill a major content void.
